Your year end is the date your accounts are drawn up to. A little preparation beforehand makes the accounts quicker, cheaper and more useful.

## Before the date

- **Bring your bookkeeping up to date.** Every sale and every cost recorded, and the bank accounts reconciled.
- **Chase what you're owed.** Invoices still unpaid at the year end need a decision: will they be paid, or written off?
- **Think about big purchases.** Buying equipment just before or just after the year end can change when you get the tax relief.

## Just after

- **Count your stock**, if you hold any, on the day or as close to it as you can.
- **List what you owe** that hasn't been invoiced yet, such as an accountant's or utility bill.
- **Send us your records** as soon as you can. The earlier we have them, the earlier you know your tax bill.

## Questions to ask

If you're not sure whether something counts as a business cost, or how a purchase will be treated, ask before the year end rather than after. It's usually easier to plan than to fix.
