A business can be profitable on paper and still run short of cash. Customers pay late, bills arrive together, and tax falls due in large amounts. These three habits help.

## 1. Look ahead, not just back

A simple forecast of the money coming in and going out over the next three months shows you a shortfall while there's still time to do something about it. A spreadsheet is enough to start.

## 2. Invoice promptly, and follow up

Send invoices as soon as the work is done, with clear payment terms. Put a reminder in your diary for the day payment is due, and chase politely the day after.

## 3. Put tax money aside

Move a share of every payment you receive into a separate account for tax. When the bill arrives, the money is already there.
